# Optical proximity sensor

* CM36653/TMD2672 occur in early-r00 light/proximity descriptions; this does
  not establish a fitted optical proximity sensor on the owner's board.
* No positively identified chip, verified current-board wiring or near/far
  acceptance exists. r02 omits the early nodes; population is **UNKNOWN**.
* Grip/SAR sensing and the Hall cover switch are different hardware functions,
  not evidence of optical proximity support.

Evidence: [Android probe data](../docs/android-hardware-observation-20261022.json), [pinned board sources](../docs/hardware-source-manifest.json).
